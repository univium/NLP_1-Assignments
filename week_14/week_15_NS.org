** Why did PaLM 2 improve over PaLM despite being smaller?
    PaLM was trained on a more diverse and high-quality dataset, including more multilingual text and source code. Furthermore, it utilized more efficient compute-optimal scaling laws, proving that better data and architecture refinement are often more effective than simply increasing parameter count.
** Why is Med-PaLM 2 considered a breakthrough in healthcare?
    It was the first LLM to perform at an "expert" level on the US Medical Licensing Examination style questions. Beyond just facts, it demonstrated an ability to align its reasoning with medical safety standards and clinician-approved consensus, which is vital for high-stakes healthcare environments.
** Why did Transformers largely replace LSTMs for large-scale NLP?
    Transformers utilize a "Self-Attention" mechanism that allows them to process entire sequences of text simultaneously rather than sequentially. This parallelization makes them much faster to train on massive datasets and allows them to capture long-range dependencies in text that LSTMs often lose.

* Part 3: LLMs for Workforce Productivity

** 1. Give two real-world workforce applications of LLMs.
In the Legal field, LLMs are used for "Contract Analysis," where they can scan thousands of pages to find conflicting clauses or summarize risks in seconds. In Education, LLMs act as "Adaptive Tutors," providing personalized feedback to students on their essays or explaining complex math problems in multiple different ways tailored to the student’s level.

** 2. How do LLMs reduce cognitive or administrative workload?
LLMs automate the "first draft" process, taking the burden of the blank page away from workers by generating reports, emails, or code snippets based on brief instructions. They also serve as powerful summarization tools, allowing professionals to digest long meetings or technical documents quickly, thereby freeing up time for high-level decision-making and creative strategy.

** 3. Why might companies still prefer smaller or open-source models like LLaMA over GPT?
Companies often prefer open-source models for Data Privacy, as they can host the model on their own local servers without sending sensitive proprietary data to a third-party cloud. Additionally, smaller models are significantly cheaper to run (lower inference costs) and can be fine-tuned on a company’s specific internal data to perform better on niche tasks than a general-purpose model.

* Part 4: Continual Learning & Responsible AI

** 1. What is continual learning, and why is it important for LLMs?
Continual learning is the ability of a model to autonomously update its knowledge over time as new information becomes available without being retrained from scratch. It is important because the world is dynamic; without it, LLMs become "frozen in time" and provide outdated or irrelevant information.

** 2. What is catastrophic forgetting, and why is it dangerous?
Catastrophic forgetting occurs when an LLM learns new information so aggressively that it completely loses or "overwrites" previously learned knowledge. This is dangerous because a model could lose its ability to perform basic reasoning or maintain safety protocols while trying to learn a specialized new skill.

** 3. Name one ethical risk of deploying LLMs and one mitigation strategy.
*** Risk: Algorithmic Bias, where the model reflects and amplifies societal prejudices found in its training data (e.g., in hiring or lending).
*** Mitigation: RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback), where human reviewers explicitly rank model outputs to discourage biased behavior and reward neutral, fair responses.
